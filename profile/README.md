<h1 align="center">CELESTIAL ELECTRIC</h1>
<p align="center"><strong>Power systems. Connected operations.</strong></p>
<p align="center">Electrical · Solar · Energy storage · Microgrids · Automation</p>

Celestial Electric LLC connects hands-on electrical expertise with purpose-built software for energy systems and field operations. Based in Hartsville, South Carolina, our work spans electrical infrastructure, solar, battery storage, microgrids, commissioning, and troubleshooting.

## What we're building

| Focus | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **CEOS** | Connected business operations, project records, and field workflows. |
| **QUASAR** | Technical analysis and owner decision support. |
| **Mobile power & monitoring** | Practical monitoring, communications, and field capability. |

These are ongoing development efforts. Each project documents its own scope, readiness, and verification evidence.

## How we work

Clear scope. Traceable changes. Independent review. Evidence before release.

We connect the field work to the record: what changed, who reviewed it, what was verified, and how to recover if something goes wrong. Software review and field commissioning each require their own evidence and approval.
