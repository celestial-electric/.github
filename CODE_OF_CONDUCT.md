# Celestial Electric code of conduct

We expect professional, respectful collaboration across our repositories and project discussions.

- Discuss the work with clear evidence. Critique an idea or implementation without attacking its author.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Explain technical concerns plainly and make room for questions.
- Protect confidential information, personal privacy, and customer records.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate another participant, or publish someone else's private information.
- Keep participation relevant to the project and respect maintainers' review and moderation decisions.

Report concerns privately to **info@celestialelectric.com** with links or a concise account of the behavior. Share only the details needed to investigate. Organization owners may request changes, remove inappropriate content, or restrict participation according to the circumstances and available platform controls.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other Celestial Electric project collaboration spaces.
